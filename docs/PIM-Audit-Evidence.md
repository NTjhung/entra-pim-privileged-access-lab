## Review Questions

During a privileged access review, an auditor or IAM reviewer may ask:

1. Who has privileged access?
2. Is the access permanent or eligible?
3. Was privileged access activated?
4. Was a justification provided?
5. How long was the access active?
6. Is there evidence of the activation?
7. Are break-glass accounts documented?

## Audit Summary

This lab demonstrates how PIM can support privileged access governance by reducing standing administrator access and providing activation evidence.

## Final Statement

Privileged Identity Management supports least privilege and audit readiness by making privileged access eligible, time-limited, justified, and reviewable.
